The Triveni Confluence

Triveni refers to the confluence of three rivers. In daily life, a balanced human experience requires integrating three distinct modes of operating: how we act in the world, how we feel and connect, and how we think and reflect.

When any single stream dominates—pure productivity without heart, emotion without discernment, or intellect without action—life feels fragmented. Here, we document experiments in diet, routine, mindset, and work to bring all three into alignment.
