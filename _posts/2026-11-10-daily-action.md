When we tie our sense of self-worth exclusively to future outcomes—a promotion, a finished project, or external validation—we surrender our mental peace to variables outside our control.

## The Trap of Outcome Dependency

Modern productivity advice often obsesses over goals. While goals provide direction, hyper-focusing on the finish line creates chronic micro-anxiety. Karma yoga reframes this relationship: action is performed for the sake of the action's integrity.

## 3 Ways to Practice Today

1. **Single-tasking with intention:** Dedicate 45 minutes to one task without switching tabs.
2. **Detaching from praise:** Complete a task with high standards even when nobody is watching.
3. **Reframing chores:** Treat cooking, cleaning, and organizing as active meditation.
